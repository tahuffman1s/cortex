Cortex now comes as an AppImage: download `Cortex-0.1.1-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable and run it. No install, no root.

```sh
chmod +x Cortex-0.1.1-x86_64.AppImage
./Cortex-0.1.1-x86_64.AppImage
```

- The AppImage carries its own Python, PyGObject, GTK 4, libadwaita and libsecret, and uses your system's GVfs, keyring, fonts and graphics drivers. It needs glibc 2.39 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40, current Arch and openSUSE Tumbleweed, and later) and GVfs's SMB support (`gvfs-smb` or `gvfs-backends`), which most desktops already have.
- Apps Cortex opens, like the file manager when you open a drive, start with your system's own libraries, not the AppImage's.
- "Connect at login" starts the AppImage file itself. Moved it? Open Cortex once and the login entry follows.
- The Flatpak manifest is gone.

The source tarball builds and installs with Meson as before.
